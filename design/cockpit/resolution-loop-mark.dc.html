t1 Shape assessment, then four marks in the workspace.json vernacular
1a Assessment of the two submitted marks, and the correction the reimaginings apply
Submitted A, tapered crescent
Reads as an ear, or a chat tail with a typing indicator. Three growing dots are the loading spinner of every product on the internet, so the mark says waiting, not resolved.
The crescent is a brush stroke with a variable weight. It has no constructible geometry, so it cannot be respecified from a rule, and it fights a system built on hairlines and pills.
The one-colour version resolves to a face. Pareidolia beats semantics every time.
At 16px the dots merge into a smear and the crescent becomes a lowercase c.
Submitted B, angular loop
The loop stops looping. It reads as an arrow or a greater-than sign, not a bounded region, so the containment idea is lost.
The tail crosses the body. A crossing is a snag that no reduction survives, and the joins alternate between mitre and kink, so the form cannot be specified.
At 16px the notch closes and the mark becomes a blob.
Structurally it is the stronger direction: angular geometry is respecifiable. It needs the crossing removed and the gap made deliberate. Mark 1d is that rescue.
Shared failures, both concepts
Four parts, three dots plus a curve, is one part too many for a mark that has to hold at 16px.
Colour carries the entire meaning: orange is declared, blue is resolved. In greyscale the mark says nothing. The cockpit's own legend states that colour reinforces only, and that shape plus the literal word carry it.
Orange and blue are a different brand. This system has one accent hue, emerald rising to aqua, on a green-tinted near-black canvas, and emerald is reserved for workspace.json attribution. A two-hue status mark cannot be built from it.
The terminal frame of the motion grammar is not distinguishable from the frame before it, so resolved has no end state.
What survives, and the rule the four marks follow
Keep the thesis: evidence converging into a bounded region, with an open gap left deliberately visible. That is the cockpit's argument and it belongs in the mark.
One container plus one payload, never more. The container's stroke style carries the resolution axis: dotted not queried, dashed unresolved, solid resolved. The payload's fill carries how much is established: hollow, part, solid.
The gap sits at the upper right in every mark, at every size, so it is recognised as a feature rather than read as a drawing error.
One colour only. The marks are legible in emerald, in white, and in greyscale, because the encoding is geometric.
