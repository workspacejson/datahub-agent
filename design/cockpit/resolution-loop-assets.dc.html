The mark is built on a 96 unit square. Bracket stems sit 16 units from the edge, arms are 15 units, stroke is 7 units. The three payload dots are radius 5.5 on the centre line at 35, 48 and 61.
Clear space is one bracket height on every side, 70 units. Nothing enters it, including the wordmark in a custom lockup.
Wordmark cap height matches the bracket height. Optical gap between mark and wordmark is 16 units at every scale.
Below 20px, drop the outer dots and set the payload as one solid bar. Below 16px, use the app icon rather than the mark.
